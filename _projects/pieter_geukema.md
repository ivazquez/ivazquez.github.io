**Current:** Master's Student - University of Amsterdam

Pieter is a Master's student in Biomedical Sciences at the University of Amsterdam. His previous research aimed to identify epigenetic immune-evasion mechanisms in gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma using patient-derived organoid and tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte co-cultures at the Amsterdam UMC, and to understand the role of immune interactions in therapy response at the Netherlands Cancer Institute. In the lab, he studies how tumor clones and the immune system co-evolve across space and time to drive treatment resistance.
