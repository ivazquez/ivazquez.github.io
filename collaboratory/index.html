Institute

Mass General Brigham Cancer Institute

Brings together cancer care and research across Mass General Brigham. Clinical care is organized into 12 services: breast, blood cancers, gastrointestinal, genitourinary, gynecologic, lung, head and neck, cutaneous, sarcoma, orthopedic oncology, pediatric hematology/oncology, and tumors of the nervous system. President: David P. Ryan.

Research center

Krantz Family Center for Cancer Research

Established with the Krantz family gift in 2023. Its 62 principal investigators hold Harvard Medical School appointments in Medicine (34), Pathology (15), Radiation Oncology (4), Surgery (3), Dermatology (2) and Pediatrics (1). Funds collaborative projects through the Krantz Awards (Quantum, Breakthrough, Spark, Technology). Director: Daniel A. Haber · Scientific Director: Raul Mostoslavsky.

Department

MGB Pathology

15 KFCCR labs, many in the Molecular Pathology Unit, sit in Pathology: genomics, single-cell and spatial methods, computational biology and molecular diagnostics. Pathology research labs outside KFCCR are not yet in the catalog.

Method

How this catalog is built

Every 2021–2026 paper led or co-authored by the 62 KFCCR principal investigators (2,253 unique papers, excluding mega-consortium papers where the PI is not among the listed authors, and papers by other researchers with the same name) was harvested from OpenAlex, read in full where open access allows (about half) or from the abstract, and parsed by Claude into datasets, cohorts and reusable resources, each with a quote from the paper as evidence. Repository accessions were checked against GEO, ENA/SRA, dbGaP, EGA, PRIDE, Zenodo and GitHub. Cancer types are grouped with OncoTree. Lab heads can correct any record from its detail panel.
