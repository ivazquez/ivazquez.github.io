Our lab together with the Haber/Maheswaran, Mishra, Miyamoto and Toner labs at MGH received a [Krantz Technology Award](https://www.massgeneral.org/cancer-center/clinical-trials-and-research/center-for-cancer-research/krantz-awards) to acquire a [cellenONE](https://www.cellenion.com/cellenone/) platform for high-throughput, image-based cell isolation and picolitre dispensing for single-cell omics workflows.
